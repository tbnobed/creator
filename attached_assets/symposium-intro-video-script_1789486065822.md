# TBN AI Symposium — Intro Film
## Production script for MiniMax Hailuo video generation

**Runtime:** 3:10 (expanded from 1:30 to cover every Seminar 2 and 3 topic without rushing)
**Aspect:** 16:9, 1080p minimum, 24 fps
**Shots:** 34 generated clips, 5–7 seconds each, assembled in post
**Deliverable:** one continuous cut with VO, music, and text overlays applied in post

---

## How to use this script

Each shot has a **generation prompt** written for MiniMax. Paste the **style prefix** below at the start of every prompt so the film holds together visually. Text on screen is added in post, not generated — video models do not render type reliably. VO is recorded separately and laid over the cut.

No shot depicts a real, identifiable person. Where a person appears they are anonymous, seen from behind, in silhouette, or at a distance. This is deliberate and matches the voice-and-likeness governance in Seminar 2.

---

## Style prefix — paste at the start of every prompt

> Cinematic broadcast facility, ultra-realistic, shot on a digital cinema camera, shallow depth of field, dark environment with deep navy and near-black tones, accents of glowing teal and soft violet light, subtle atmospheric haze, clean and modern, no visible text, no logos, no identifiable faces, slow deliberate camera movement, 24 fps, filmic grain.

**Consistency rules across all shots:** teal is always the colour of live signal and measured data; violet is always the colour of AI inference. Keep every light source motivated — monitors, LED walls, rack LEDs, fibre. Never flat-lit.

---

## Music and VO direction

**Music:** one continuous bed. Opens sparse — a low sustained synth pad with a slow pulse at roughly 70 bpm. Builds through the plant and engineering acts with layered percussion. Pulls back to the pad for governance. Resolves on the close. No lyrics. Reference feel: restrained, confident, institutional rather than hype.

**VO:** single voice, warm and unhurried, mid-register. Not a movie-trailer read. The tone is someone explaining what their team built, not selling it. Recorded dry, light room tone, no processing beyond EQ.

## Post-production notes

**Transitions:** cuts throughout, no dissolves except the final fade. Shot 17 to 18 should be a hard cut on the LED going red.

**Text overlays:** one typeface, all caps, small, lower-left, teal for measured facts and violet for anything the AI inferred (matches the platform's colour system). Never centred, never large. Text should feel like a caption, not a title card — except the two TBN AI SYMPOSIUM cards, which sit centre and hold.

**Colour:** grade every generated clip to the same reference — lift the teal, keep violet soft, crush nothing. Generated clips will vary; a unified grade is what makes thirty-four of them read as one film.

**Audio:** VO sits slightly above the music throughout. Leave two beats of silence after "Let me break something" before shot 18's VO begins.

**Running time check:** 34 shots totalling 3:10 including the final fade. If it must come down toward two minutes, cut shots 5, 10, 20, 26 and 28 in that order — the remaining cut still covers every topic in both seminars, just with less breathing room.
